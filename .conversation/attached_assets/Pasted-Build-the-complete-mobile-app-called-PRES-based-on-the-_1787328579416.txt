==================================================

1. APP CONCEPT
   ==================================================

PRES is a university party/pres game app for UK university students.

The purpose is to make choosing and playing drinking/party games easier when you're with friends.

The app should feel:

* Fast
* Fun
* Social
* Very easy to use
* Playful
* Slightly chaotic
* Designed specifically for university students

The user should be able to open PRES, choose what type of pres they are having, pick a game and immediately start playing.

Most games are played physically in real life. PRES primarily provides:

* Questions
* Prompts
* Timers
* Random selections
* Game instructions
* Simple interaction
* Game progression

Do NOT make every game require players to constantly interact with the phone.

13. BACK TO BACK
================

Two players sit back-to-back physically.

The app shows a question such as:

“Who is more likely to pull tonight?”

Both players point physically.

Then they turn around.

The app can show:

“MATCHED 😭”

or:

“NOT EVEN CLOSE 💀”

Allow the user to select the two players.

Do not require them to make their actual choice through the app.

==================================================
14. PARANOIA
============

Show:

“ONLY YOU SHOULD LOOK 👀”

Example:

Afterwards optionally allow:

“Reveal Question”

Make sure the question cannot accidentally be displayed to the whole group.
